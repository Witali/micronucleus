## Source and protocol

The handoff archive and its 148 manifest entries were checked before import;
provenance, intermediate sizes and rejected controls are in
[the integration record](PASS40_IMPORT.md). Shared EP0/Flash reductions total
16 bytes, and shared PHY rejection/ACK tails save eight more. Additional
exact-timing sharing saves six bytes in the corrected sampler and four bytes
in final publication. The latter preserves parser clocks, registers/SREG,
SRAM, stack and input observations across 2048 states; its remaining 872 linked
instructions and 261 relative edges also match. Public
SELECT/ERASE/WRITE4/READ8/RUN bytes and application layout rules are preserved.
The source remains without LED and without permanent EEPROM commands.

The repair closes several distinct faults:

- Commit a byte before the post-bit7-stuffer bit0 observation. That observation
  moves two clocks; later normal samples retain their established timing.
- Schedule existing correction/fraction work before reading a mandatory bit7
  zero, moving the observation three clocks later without adding instructions.
- Resume verified mandatory transitions after short odd1/3/5 crossover zeros.
  A short zero is not allowed to publish a packet or release old ACK ownership.
- After the maximum eleven-byte packet, preserve final CRC symbol timing and
  use an additional early SE0 observation. It does not count a dribble bit.
  Defer the final numeric CRC store until qualified EOP; both CRC positions
  and the complete PID/payload count remain required.
- Bound final ones with the shifted FE mask. The 10/10/12-clock loop keeps its
  fractional timing; auxiliary probes detect short EOP without adding tail bits.
  Two full SE0 observations four clocks apart exclude one 210 ns excursion;
  the later adjacent-pulse finding below invalidates a general EOP proof.

Only incoming numeric DATA CRC16 validation and storage are waived. Outgoing
CRC16, token CRC5, exact PID/address/endpoint, mandatory stuffing, full packet
framing, response timing, retry/ACK ownership and Flash guards remain checked.

## Open mandatory asymmetric crossover

The exact 1788-byte body drops DATA `c3fffffffffffffffffe70` at nominal
1500000 bit/s, zero differential jitter, handler entry eight clocks, transition
index 11 (PID bit 3), and a 210 ns both-low interval from -10 to +200 ns around
the differential crossing. It does not receive the complete packet or ACK it.
Pristine and centered-210 ns controls pass. The first GPIO observation sees SE0
at 120.4999999 clocks; confirmation four clocks later sees K, and the shared
ordinary even/odd EOP-confirmation path rejects the recovered transition.

One common-2 V threshold witness uses these piecewise-linear voltage points.
Add rail endpoints at -70/+250 ns and clamp outside them:

| Line | Time in ns, voltage in V |
| --- | --- |
| Rising D+ | (-70,0), (-60,0.33), (0,1.3), (200,2), (240,2.97), (250,3.3) |
| Falling D- | (-70,3.3), (-60,2.97), (-10,2), (0,1.3), (240,0.33), (250,0) |

Each line and the differential transition has a 300 ns 10..90% interval;
the edges are monotonic, exactly matched and cross uniquely at 1.3 V at time
zero. USB2.0 section 7.1.2.1 requires those limits, while equal local slew and
constant common mode are only preferred. Section 7.1.13.1.1 measures data jitter
at differential crossover points, which this construction leaves unchanged.
Thus the input consumes no extra differential-jitter budget. The 210 ns
single-ended interval is within section 7.1.4.1. This is a mandatory receive
finding, not a numeric-CRC waiver or an unspecified-input allowance.

The reproducer, fresh-buffer classification and witness proof are preserved in
the local `validation/endpoint-crossover-nov01/` record. The maintained
`test_rx_crossover_asymmetric` adds a strict 55th group. Its 2376 single-crossing
cases plus 108 cases with all 22 data crossings affected simultaneously produce
120 failures on the frozen 1788-byte control: 94 single and 26 repeated. Both
mirrored voltage witnesses are checked arithmetically; each source waveform
receives an independent next/paired jitter certificate. The immutable baseline
is archived alongside the 54-group checkpoint as `asymmetric-baseline-2484.json`.
Repair is in progress. The 1788-byte image is a control checkpoint, not a
qualified release candidate.

The 56th group, `test_rx_crossover_payloads`, starts with 978 nominal-rate asymmetric
cases for 00/15/9F payloads, including all-transition repetitions and three
pristine controls. The frozen 1788-byte body fails 66 injected cases; a private
1900-byte odd-recovery control reduces that subset to 16, while its 2484-case
rate/jitter gate still fails 28. A private 1920-byte odd/even composition reduces
the latter to 16 but remains unqualified. Recovered line state can describe a
future transition rather than the current data bit; the code must preserve
payload bits, not infer them solely to fit the all-FF corpus. Consecutive legal
transients also require recovery, even when a preceding decoded zero rules
out legal dribble. These controls are diagnostic evidence, not admitted images.

The payload gate now has 981 injected cases and six pristine controls. It also
retains both sides of an exact observation alias: EF repeated eight times at
fast host/sign-1/entry8 with a next-stuffed-transition pulse, and EF repeated
seven times plus DB at slow host/sign+1/entry5 with a current-transition pulse.
The private 1920-byte body has identical registers, SREG, RX/context memory and
stack at its recovery decision, yet the intended final payload bit2 differs.
It ACKs the first signal while publishing DB instead of EF. No guard based only
on that existing state can choose both correctly. New observation or phase
information is necessary; the numeric CRC waiver does not cover this corruption.

The third directed payload case combines differently placed adjacent pulses.
Two SE0 reads four clocks apart may belong to separate legal transitions.
Explicit rail tails settle both analog waveforms between adjacent crossings.
The private 1920-byte body fails 18 of the current 981 injected cases; all six
pristine controls pass.

The token-admission gate now adds 54 adjacent-pulse cases and 54 matching

## Rejected reductions

- Universal early storage fixes some DATA cases but introduces valid IN-token
  jitter failures. A DATA-only tail sweep was insufficient qualification.
- Removing the final-byte fractional schedule fixes one short-EOP family but
  breaks 69 other legal DATA waveforms and can accept an invalid FC ending.
- A constant eleven-clock tail loop accepts seven unstuffed final ones at a
  specific fast-host/jitter phase. A ten-clock loop rejects legal six-one tails.
- Merging ordinary and odd5 terminal history tests drops 90 of 450 valid CRC
  ending cases despite preserving the final bit7 sample clock.
- Coalescing another poll saves two bytes but raises worst foreground entry by
  seven clocks; that wider entry range is not admitted without qualification.

The smaller-loop counterexample is retained as a strict regression. No failure
was converted to an expected result or hidden by changing the CRC waiver.

These tests execute compiled AVR instructions with digital GPIO timing. Analog
thresholds, loading, oscillator error on an actual board and electrical USB
certification remain outside this software qualification.
